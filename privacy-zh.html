Device Permissions Required by the Application

To function properly as a reliable alarm clock, the Application requires the following system permissions. We only use these permissions to provide the Application's core functionality, and no personal data is ever collected, stored, or transmitted through them:

  • Exact Alarm permission (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM): used to ensure alarms ring at the exact time you set.
  • Notification permission: used to display the alarm's active status in the status bar and to trigger alerts.
  • Battery optimization settings: the Application opens the system's battery optimization list so that you can manually exclude the Application, preventing the Android system from putting the Application to sleep, so that alarms continue to ring reliably even while the device is in deep sleep.
  • System alert window / display over other apps: used to display the full-screen alarm dismiss interface over the lock screen or other running applications when an alarm rings, and to display the bedtime reminder popup at your configured bedtime.
  • Full-screen intent permission (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): used to launch the alarm-ringing screen directly over the lock screen when an alarm fires, so you see it immediately rather than only receiving a notification.
  • Foreground service permission (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): used to ensure the alarm sound can keep playing reliably in the background while the alarm is ringing.
  • Receive Boot Completed permission (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): used to automatically reschedule your alarms after the device restarts, without you needing to reopen the Application.

To provide weather, currency, and news content, the city name, currency code, and your selected app language are sent to the Service Provider's own backend service. The city name you enter is processed the same way: it is stored on the backend only as a cryptographically hashed (non-reversible) cache key, used solely to avoid repeating the same request to the third-party weather provider, and is never stored in a form that could be read back as the city you typed.


Device Permissions Required

To function properly as a reliable alarm clock, the Application requires the following system permissions. The Service Provider only uses these permissions to provide the core functionality of the app, and no personal data from them is ever collected, stored, or transmitted:

  • Exact Alarm (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to trigger your alarms precisely at the exact second you have set them.
  • Notification Permission: Required to display the active alarm status in your status bar and to trigger alerts.
  • Battery Optimization Settings: The app opens the system's battery optimization list so you can manually exclude it, preventing the Android OS from putting the app to sleep and ensuring that your alarms will ring reliably even if the device is in deep sleep.
  • System Alert Window / Draw Over Other Apps: Required to display the full-screen alarm dismiss interface over your lock screen or other running Applications when an alarm goes off, and to display the bedtime reminder popup at your configured bedtime.
  • Full-Screen Intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): Required to launch the alarm-ringing screen directly over your lock screen when an alarm fires, so you see it immediately instead of only a notification.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Required to keep the alarm sound playing reliably in the background while the alarm is ringing.
  • Receive Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Required to automatically re-schedule your alarms after your device restarts, so they continue to work without you needing to reopen the app.
