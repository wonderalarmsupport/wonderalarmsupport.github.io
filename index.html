Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Wonder Alarm app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by AlphaMu Studios (collectively, the "Application"). AlphaMu Studios is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


Information Collection and Use

The Application does not directly collect personal data. However, information may be collected automatically through third-party services when you download and use the Application.

Your alarms, custom labels, custom alarm sounds, sleep logs, and water and stretch tracking data are stored only locally on your device and are never transmitted to the Service Provider or to any third party.

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address
  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • Application crash logs, non-fatal error messages and performance analytics
  • Your mobile operating system you use

The Application does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. However, it processes device-related identifiers and usage data through third-party services for analytics, advertising, and crash reporting purposes.

The Application also allows you to select a city, a base currency, and an in-app display language. To provide weather, currency, and news content, the city name, currency code, and your selected app language are sent to the Service Provider's own backend service. For news specifically, your device's region and system language — detected automatically from your device settings, not separately selected within the app — are also sent, so that region-appropriate headlines can be retrieved. In every case, only these necessary values are forwarded — never any personally identifiable information — to third-party weather, currency, and news data providers in order to retrieve and display this content. News thumbnail images, when available, are loaded directly from the original publisher's own servers, which is standard practice for news and RSS readers; this means your device connects directly to that publisher when displaying such an image.

The Service Provider's own backend service also necessarily receives your device's IP address with every request, which it uses briefly and only in a cryptographically hashed (non-reversible) form to apply rate limiting and protect the service against abuse; this record is automatically deleted within a few minutes. The city name you enter is processed the same way: it is stored on the backend only as a cryptographically hashed (non-reversible) cache key, used solely to avoid repeating the same request to the third-party weather provider, and is never stored in a form that could be read back as the city you typed.


Device Permissions Required

To function properly as a reliable alarm clock, the Application requires the following system permissions. The Service Provider only uses these permissions to provide the core functionality of the app, and no personal data from them is ever collected, stored, or transmitted:

  • Exact Alarm (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to trigger your alarms precisely at the exact second you have set them.
  • Notification Permission: Required to display the active alarm status in your status bar and to trigger alerts.
  • Battery Optimization Settings: The app opens the system's battery optimization list so you can manually exclude it, preventing the Android OS from putting the app to sleep and ensuring that your alarms will ring reliably even if the device is in deep sleep.
  • System Alert Window / Draw Over Other Apps: Required to display the full-screen alarm dismiss interface over your lock screen or other running Applications when an alarm goes off, and to display the bedtime reminder popup at your configured bedtime.
  • Full-Screen Intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): Required to launch the alarm-ringing screen directly over your lock screen when an alarm fires, so you see it immediately instead of only a notification.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Required to keep the alarm sound playing reliably in the background while the alarm is ringing.
  • Receive Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Required to automatically re-schedule your alarms after your device restarts, so they continue to work without you needing to reopen the app.
